Our Story

Founded on January 1, 2025 in Dornbirn, Vorarlberg, Austria — Not Another Mate Software brings together 25 years of combined experience in software development and film production with cutting-edge Generative AI technology. Our team's deep domain knowledge from both worlds flows directly into every product we build.

Our Mission

We develop GenAI SaaS products that give creative professionals the tools they deserve. Our philosophy: the best AI tools are born when deep domain knowledge from film and video meets excellent software engineering. We don't automate creativity — we amplify it.

Our Location

Our headquarters are nestled in the Alps of Vorarlberg, Austria. From here, we serve clients worldwide — in German and English.
